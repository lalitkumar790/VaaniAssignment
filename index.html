03 / Engineering judgment

What the measurements changed.

The design deliberately gives up some throughput and completeness to avoid speculative or uncited answers.

Where the time goes

Request traces distinguish retrieval/planning, provider response, first model content and post-content verification. The model's internal first token is not the first fully verified user-visible answer. The two-page report dissects a slow V5 request.

Limits, shedding and retries

At minute limits, bounded queueing or rejection protects latency; at day exhaustion, hosted calls stop and the person receives an explicit unavailable/refusal response. Retry a transient pre-token failure once when the deadline allows; never replay streamed partial output.

Retrieval decision

Three settings trade additional context tokens for slightly better strict correctness. Balanced (500-character chunks, top 3, lexical reranking) is the chosen cost/quality compromise. Near token limits, reduce context size before weakening citation checks.

What can go wrong

“Attention heads” can retrieve the wrong paper; Moshi's 160 ms theoretical, 200 ms practical and 640 ms incompatible delay can be confused; H-Net's learned chunking differs from fixed retrieval text windows. The guard checks citation labels and numerics but not all semantic claims.
