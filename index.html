04 / Engineering policy

What happens at the limit?

Per-minute exhaustion

Admit up to the current RPM/TPM budget. Refuse new hosted work when the deadline can't accommodate a retry-after wait. Do not pretend a 429 is a normal answer. Queueing has a bounded budget.

admit
retrieve
stream
verify

Retry only when no answer tokens have been streamed and the deadline still allows it.

Daily budget exhausted

Stop sending requests to Groq. Explain that verified hosted answers are unavailable for the day instead of silently switching to a weaker answer. Where evidence is absent, refuse without a model call.

“I can’t give you a verified response right now because today’s model quota is exhausted. Please try again after the quota resets.”
